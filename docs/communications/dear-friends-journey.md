# Dear friends

Dear friends,

This is the beginning of a journey that started a long time ago. A journey of discovery, failure, and successes, all of varying degrees.

Sometimes I’ve looked back on experiences I thought were total disasters, and through some maturing reflection come to see them as deeply rewarding — the kind of thing that turns out to mean something after all.

So many times walking up and down the aisles of businesses, talking to owners about how they want to go about production, I’ve realized that what I’m building is the tooling I’ve always wanted. Something I could sling over my shoulder with my notebooks and pencils. A tool that could capture what clients are saying as they describe how they’ve seen their factory evolve — in their mind and in their dreams. Honor that complexity, and the teams they’ve put together around it. Break the complex down into the repetitive patterns, instructions, materials, and tools. Without pretending the hard parts aren’t hard.

What I want to share with you is that tool. It’s for the work I do: floors, owners, teams, turning a factory vision into something you can actually run — lines, materials, instructions, decisions. I built it for myself. I hope other people who do this work will find it as useful as I am expecting it to be.

I think best in pictures and diagrams. Over the coming weeks and months I want to share some of that — how I’m building tools that help put production lines together, and the clear information teams and owners need for decisions, strategy, budgets, and execution.

Thanks for listening, and for taking a look.

Regards,  
Michael Carroll
